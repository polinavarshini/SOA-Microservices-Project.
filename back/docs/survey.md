# Design Thinking Validation Survey

**IMPORTANT**: Survey responses must be collected from actual respondents and entered here before final submission. Do not fabricate responses.

## Survey Objectives
To validate the necessity and effectiveness of the Automated Commercial Insurance Claim Verification & Settlement Engine prototype among target demographics (Insurance professionals and policyholders).

## Questionnaire Template

**1. Demographic Information**
- Are you a Policyholder or an Insurance Industry Professional?

**2. Current Frustrations**
- On a scale of 1-5, how frustrating is the current manual process for verifying commercial insurance claims?

**3. Verification Delays**
- What is the primary cause of delays in your current claim document verification process?

**4. Transparency & Tracking**
- How useful would a centralized dashboard be for real-time tracking of claim statuses (`SUBMITTED` -> `APPROVED` -> `SETTLED`)? (1-5 Scale)

**5. Automated Settlements**
- Would an automated mathematical calculation of final payouts (Claim - Deductible) increase your trust in the insurance provider?

**6. Security Concerns**
- Does a microservice architecture (where your identity, policies, and claims are isolated in different databases) alleviate data privacy concerns? 

**7. Overall Feedback**
- What is one feature you would add to an automated claim system?
